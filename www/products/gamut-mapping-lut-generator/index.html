The Challenge

Why HDR looks flat in SDR

Rec.2020 covers ~75% of the visible spectrum — nearly twice the gamut of Rec.709. Standard LUTs crush saturated colors, clip highlights, and introduce banding. The result: deliverables that don't match your grade.

Our CIEDE2000-aware engine preserves perceptual color accuracy across the entire gamut compression, so your creative intent survives the transform.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Gamut-Mapping LUT Generator.

What versions of DaVinci Resolve are supported? ▼
DaVinci Resolve 17 and above (Studio required for external LUT support). The generated .cube files are compatible with both the Color Page and the OFX LUT node.
Does it work with other NLEs? ▼
Yes. Exports standard .cube format compatible with Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, Baselight, Nuke, Scratch, and any application that supports 3D LUTs.
What's the difference between Standard and Pro? ▼
Pro adds support for all color spaces (ACEScct, DCI-P3, Sony S-Log, Canon C-Log, ARRI Log C, Panasonic V-Log), 65×65×65 LUT resolution, batch processing, CLI API access, and priority support.
Is it a one-time purchase or subscription? ▼
One-time purchase. Your license includes 1 year of free updates. After the first year, you can optionally renew for continued updates at a reduced rate. The software continues to work without renewal.
What source color spaces are supported? ▼
Standard tier supports Rec.2020, Rec.709, and DCI-P3. Pro adds ACEScct, Sony S-Log3/S-Log2, Canon C-Log/C-Log2/C-Log3, ARRI Log C (wide gamut), Panasonic V-Log/V-Gamut, and RED IPP2. We're constantly adding new spaces based on user requests.
